# areas/content/

Anything published externally: website, social channels, newsletter, and
other public-facing output.

## What lives here

One file or subfolder per channel (e.g. `website.md`, `social/`,
`newsletter.md`).

## Conventions

- Name files after the channel, not the campaign or individual post.
- Keep voice, style, and publishing conventions in the channel's own file
  so they stay consistent across contributors.
- Published pieces: log what went out and when, rather than keeping only
  drafts.

## Live status

What is scheduled, in review, or just published lives in `memory/` (see
`memory/CLAUDE.md`). This area holds the channel conventions, memory holds
what is live right now.
